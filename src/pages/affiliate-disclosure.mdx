## The commission

If you buy a MailCheap plan through a link on this site, we may earn a commission from MailCheap. The price you pay is the price on MailCheap’s pricing page. We do not add a fee. We do not control MailCheap’s plans, caps, or refunds.

The affiliate code is one value in the site configuration: `analyst`. Pricing links look like `https://mailcheap.ai/pricing?via=analyst`. A link to the MailCheap home page uses the same code on `https://mailcheap.ai/?via=analyst`. Citation links to MailCheap help articles do not include the code.

We do not run coupon codes, private offers, or “deal” pages. If a vendor’s ordinary yearly price is lower than twelve monthly payments, we describe that billing term. We do not invent a discount.

Links to beehiiv, Kit, Ghost, Substack, Mailchimp, and MailerLite on this site are not affiliate links. They go to those companies’ sites so you can check a price.

## How to spot a link

Affiliate links use `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` and open in a new tab. The calculator marks the MailCheap row with an Affiliate tag. Buttons say when they are affiliate links, in the line under the button. The bar at the top of every page repeats the disclosure in short form.

If a link is not marked that way, we do not intend it to be an affiliate link. If you find one that should be marked and is not, email [hello@sendforless.com](mailto:hello@sendforless.com).

## Independence

sendforless is not MailCheap. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, partnered with, or speaking for MailCheap, except for the ordinary fact of an affiliate program. We are not an official MailCheap site, review desk, or support channel.

Recommendations are editorial. MailCheap is named when a regular sender is comparing paid tools and does not need a free hosted plan, paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests. When a free plan fits, we say use the free plan. The commission does not move a $0 row below a $19 row in the advice, even when the chart sorts $0 to the top on price alone.
