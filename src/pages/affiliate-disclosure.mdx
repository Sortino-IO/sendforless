## The commission

If you buy a MailCheap plan through a link on this site, we may earn a commission from MailCheap. The price you pay is the price on MailCheap’s pricing page. We do not add a fee. We do not control MailCheap’s plans, caps, or refunds.

We do not run coupon codes, private offers, or deal pages. If a vendor’s ordinary yearly price is lower than twelve monthly payments, we describe that billing term. We do not invent a discount.

Links to beehiiv, Kit, Ghost, Substack, Mailchimp, MailerLite, and Flodesk go to those companies’ sites so you can check a price. They are not affiliate links from sendforless.

## How to spot a link

Affiliate links are labeled in the line under the button, open in a new tab, and are marked as sponsored for search engines. The calculator tags the MailCheap row as Affiliate. The bar at the top of every page repeats the disclosure.

Citations of a vendor’s help article are ordinary links. They are there so you can read the source. If a link should be labeled and is not, email [hello@sendforless.com](mailto:hello@sendforless.com).

## Independence

sendforless is not MailCheap. We are not an official MailCheap site, review desk, or support channel. The commission does not add features MailCheap does not sell. We still recommend a free plan when it already sends your newsletter.
