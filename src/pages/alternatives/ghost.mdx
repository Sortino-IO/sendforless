## Alternatives

**Substack** if you want $0 until readers pay and you can live with 10% after that. You give up theme control.

**beehiiv** if you want a hosted publication with recommendations and ads. Lite at 10,000 subscribers is $139, against Publisher at $105. The products are not the same stack. [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/).

**Kit** if the list is a creator funnel. Free to 10,000 for broadcasts. [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/).

**MailerLite** if you want pages and automations without running Ghost. Comfort at 10,000 subscribers is $89. Ghost's member bands are on [Ghost pricing](/pricing/ghost/). The percent they replace is on [Substack pricing](/pricing/substack/).

**MailCheap** if the Ghost site was a thin wrapper around a send and you do not sell memberships. A weekly letter to 10,000 subscribers is $39 on Growth. You lose the theme, the member portal, and the 0% commerce. [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/).
