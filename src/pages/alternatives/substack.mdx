  <h2>Stay while 10% is smaller than a monthly plan. Move when you want the fee back and you can replace the network.</h2>
  <p>Owning the list is the easy part: Substack lets you export. Replacing recommendations inside the Substack app is not. Price the fee with your revenue, then decide if a flat plan’s missing network is acceptable.</p>
</section>

## When to stay

Stay if most readers are free and you do not want a monthly bill for them. Stay if paid revenue is under the beehiiv Lite or Ghost Publisher tier at your member count. Stay if the app is where new readers come from and you have not found another source. The comparison with the closest paid-publication rival is [beehiiv vs Substack](/compare/beehiiv-vs-substack/).

## Alternatives

**beehiiv** charges the subscriber bucket and takes 0% of paid subscriptions on Lite and Pro. Free stops at 2,500. Lite at 4,000 subscribers (the 5,000 bucket) is $109 a month. You get recommendations, and ads on paid plans where beehiiv offers them. [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/).

**Ghost(Pro)** is the membership site. Publisher starts at $35 a month at 1,000 members and takes 0%. There is no hosted free plan. [Ghost vs Substack](/compare/ghost-vs-substack/).

**Kit** is $0 up to 10,000 subscribers for broadcasts and can sell products. It is not a Substack-style public network. [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/).

**MailerLite Comfort** can sell a paid newsletter from $12 a month at 500 subscribers. At 5,000 subscribers Comfort is $49.

**MailCheap** sends the letter and does not sell subscriptions. It fits a free publication that will charge readers somewhere else (a course, a separate membership, a sponsor) and wants a known monthly cap. Starter is $19. It does not fit if you might turn on paid subscriptions inside the newsletter this year.

## Leaving

Export subscribers with all columns. Point a domain you control at the new host before you announce the move. If readers pay, recreate the offer and run a test charge, including your own card, before you turn the old paywall off. Tell paying readers twice. MailCheap cannot take those subscriptions. Do not export to MailCheap until you have another way to charge them.
