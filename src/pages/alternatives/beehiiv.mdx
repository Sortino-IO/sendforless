## Alternatives

**Kit** for sequences and a longer free broadcast plan. [beehiiv vs Kit](/compare/beehiiv-vs-convertkit/).

**Substack** for a percent of revenue instead of a tier, if you can live in its network.

**Ghost** for a membership site you theme. [Ghost alternatives](/alternatives/ghost/) if you are choosing the site, not the exit from beehiiv.

**MailerLite Comfort** if you want automations and a paid newsletter without beehiiv’s ad exchange. At 10,000 subscribers Comfort is $89 and Lite is $139. Comfort’s email cap is 100,000 at that tier, enough for a weekly issue and not for a daily one.

**MailCheap** for the send alone. The feature gap is the point of [MailCheap vs beehiiv](/compare/mailcheap-vs-beehiiv/). For who beehiiv suits, read the [beehiiv review](/reviews/beehiiv/).
