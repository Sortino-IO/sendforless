## Prices we can cite

The full notes are [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/). Short version, 24 November 2026:

- Free: $0, up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited broadcasts. Sequences need a paid plan.
- Creator: $39 a month up to 1,000 subscribers. Kit’s billing help uses $59 a month for the up-to-3,000 tier.
- Pro: $79 a month at 1,000 subscribers on the pricing page.
- Above those stops: [check current pricing](https://kit.com/pricing).

## Alternatives

**beehiiv** if you want recommendations and ads. Free stops at 2,500, a quarter of Kit’s 10,000. Lite at 10,000 subscribers is $139. [beehiiv vs Kit](/compare/beehiiv-vs-convertkit/).

**MailCheap** for a weekly broadcast past the free cap. Growth is $39 at 10,000 subscribers. It does not include visual sequences or the Creator Network. At 4,000 subscribers you are still on Kit Free, and MailCheap’s $19 is not a reason to move. [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/).

**Ghost(Pro)** when the newsletter is a membership site. Publisher at 10,000 members is $105.

**Substack** when you want the fee to be a percent of revenue. Publishing stays $0 at list sizes where Kit Free has ended.

**MailerLite and Mailchimp** are rarely a discount versus Kit Free. Comfort at 10,000 subscribers is $89. Mailchimp’s price above 500 contacts is on Mailchimp’s site. [Kit vs Mailchimp](/compare/convertkit-vs-mailchimp/).
