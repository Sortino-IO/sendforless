## The current prices

From [MailerLite’s pricing file](https://www.mailerlite.com/pricing.md). The review, which is about whether to stay, is the [MailerLite review](/reviews/mailerlite/). The Mailchimp comparison is [MailerLite vs Mailchimp](/compare/mailerlite-vs-mailchimp/).

| Subscribers, up to | Comfort | Comfort emails | Power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | $19 | 10,000 | $39 |
| 5,000 | $49 | 50,000 | $69 |
| 10,000 | $89 | 100,000 | $129 |
| 25,000 | $179 | 250,000 | $239 |

A daily send often needs Power because Comfort’s email cap is a multiple of the tier. A weekly send to 5,000 people (about 21,700 emails) fits Comfort’s 50,000.

## Alternatives

**Kit Free** to 10,000 subscribers for broadcasts. Creator is $39 a month up to 1,000 if you need sequences. [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/).

**MailCheap** for a weekly send without MailerLite’s paid newsletters or tests. Starter $19, Growth $39, Pro $79. At 4,000 subscribers, five weekly issues use all 20,000 Starter emails before welcomes. [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/).

**beehiiv** if you want recommendations and an ad network. Lite at 10,000 subscribers is $139. [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/).

**Ghost(Pro)** if members log into a site. Publisher at 10,000 members is $105.

**Substack** if you want $0 until readers pay, then 10%.
