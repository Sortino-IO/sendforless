## How do you use these alternatives?

Each roundup starts from one product and prices the neighbors with month-to-month USD list prices checked on November 25, 2026. Free plans are real options. Don't pay $19 a month when Kit or beehiiv sends the same weekly issue for $0.

The price pages behind these roundups are [MailerLite](/pricing/mailerlite/), [Mailchimp](/pricing/mailchimp/), [Ghost](/pricing/ghost/), [Substack](/pricing/substack/), [beehiiv](/pricing/beehiiv/), [Kit](/pricing/convertkit/), and [Flodesk](/pricing/flodesk/). Pair-by-pair comparisons are on the [comparisons hub](/compare/).

MailCheap shows up when you send on a schedule, you are past the free caps, and you do not need paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests. If the account is a store, a membership site, or a recommendation network, read the [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/) before you switch. [How we compare prices](/how-we-compare/) is the source list.
