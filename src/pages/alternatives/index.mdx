## How to read an alternatives page

Each roundup starts from one product people already use, then prices the neighbors with month-to-month USD list prices checked on 24 November 2026. Free plans are treated as real options. A $19 sender is not a recommendation when Kit or beehiiv will send the same weekly issue for $0.

MailCheap shows up where a regular, price-sensitive sender does not need paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests. It does not show up as the default escape from every logo. If your account is really a store, a membership site, or a recommendation network, the cheaper row is the wrong row. The [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/) is the short version of that boundary, and [how we test](/how-we-test/) is the source list behind the numbers.
