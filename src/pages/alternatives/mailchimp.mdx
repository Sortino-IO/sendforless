Mailchimp alternatives in 2026 are a list-size decision, not a brand ranking. The Mailchimp alternatives below are priced only where the vendor publishes the dollar. Mailchimp charges for contacts you store. Subscribed, unsubscribed, and non-subscribed people all count until you archive, clean, or delete them. A cheaper invoice only helps if the next tool still does the job: a store, a membership site, a sequence, or a plain weekly letter. Prices below were checked on vendor pages on 25 November 2026. Mailchimp's own tier above 500 contacts stays "check current pricing."

## When should you stay on Mailchimp?

Stay when Mailchimp runs the store. Journeys tied to purchases, audiences built from a connected shop, and ads that use those audiences do not move with a CSV. Rebuilding that stack on a sender that has no store connection can cost more than the contact tier, even when the newsletter line looks high.

Stay when the stored list is still inside a published start. Essentials starts at $13 a month and Standard at $20 a month, both for up to 500 contacts, on the marketing pricing page checked 25 November 2026. Premium starts at $350 a month, and the help article says that base includes 10,000 contacts. If your real tier is still the $13 or $20 start, most paid alternatives are not cheaper. Kit (ConvertKit) Free can still be $0 if you only broadcast.

Leave when you are paying for people you do not mail. Archive them first and read the new tier. If the bill is still a contact ladder you do not need, the alternatives below are the ones with a published price at a given list size.

## Free Mailchimp alternatives

Free Mailchimp alternatives that can still send are Kit, beehiiv, and Substack. Each free cap is different, and each one leaves something out.

**Kit (ConvertKit) Free** is $0 up to 10,000 subscribers for unlimited broadcasts, forms, landing pages, tagging, and segmentation. The free plan help article, dated 23 September 2026 and re-checked 25 November 2026, says visual automations, sequences, rules, and RSS need a paid plan. Creator starts at $39 a month for up to 1,000 subscribers when you need sequences, and Kit's billing help uses $59 a month as the Creator price up to 3,000. Pro is $79 a month at 1,000 subscribers on the pricing page. Above those published points, check current pricing. Details are on [Kit (ConvertKit) pricing](/pricing/convertkit/) and [Kit vs Mailchimp](/compare/convertkit-vs-mailchimp/).

**beehiiv Free** is $0 up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends and the recommendation network. Automations, paid subscriptions, and the ad network start on Lite, not on Free. Lite is $59 a month at the up-to-1,000 bucket, or $49 a month if you pay annually, and $109 a month at the up-to-5,000 bucket. The ladder is [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/).

**Substack** is $0 to publish at any list size. Paid subscriptions cost 10% of subscription revenue plus Stripe. A custom domain is $50 once. There is no contact tier. See [Substack pricing](/pricing/substack/).

Mailchimp's own free plan is the fourth $0 option, and it is the smallest. It stops at 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with a daily cap of 250. If that still covers the letter, you do not need an alternative yet.

Flodesk's free plan includes forms and landing pages. The pricing FAQ says it does not include email sending. It is not a free sending alternative.

**MailerLite Comfort** is the all-rounder with a published ladder. It starts at $12 a month for 500 subscribers and is $19 at 1,000 and $49 at 5,000. Emails on Comfort are 10 times the tier ceiling. Power starts at $25 and removes that cap, up to 200,000 subscribers, subject to fair use. Annual billing is 10% less than monthly. See [MailerLite vs Mailchimp](/compare/mailerlite-vs-mailchimp/) and [MailerLite pricing](/pricing/mailerlite/).

**MailCheap** meters emails and caps subscribers on each plan. Starter is $19 for 20,000 emails and 10,000 subscribers. Growth is $39 for 75,000 emails and 40,000 subscribers. Pro is $79 for 200,000 emails and 100,000 subscribers. A weekly letter to 4,000 people is about 17,300 emails, which fits Starter. A month with five weekly sends is 20,000 emails before welcomes and tests, which uses the whole Starter allowance. There is no hosted free plan. Paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, and A/B tests are not in the product. The write-up is the [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/). The button below is an affiliate link.

**Ghost(Pro)** fits when members log into a site you theme. Publisher is $35 a month at 1,000 members and $75 at the up-to-5,000 band. Starter is $18 a month up to 1,000 members and cannot charge readers. There is no hosted free plan. The platform fee on paid subscriptions is 0%. Stripe still applies.

**Constant Contact** is the other contact-priced marketing suite. Lite starts at $12 a month, Standard at $35, and Premium at $80, and the price then moves with contacts and sends. There is no free sending plan. A trial is free, and new plans have a 30-day refund. The pair is [Constant Contact vs Mailchimp](/compare/constant-contact-vs-mailchimp/).

Cheaper than Mailchimp is not automatic. At 500 contacts, Essentials at $13 can be less than Flodesk at $46 and less than Creator at $39. Alternatives to Mailchimp win on price when the list is past the free caps of Kit or beehiiv and you are not using the store. Mailchimp competitors that publish a full ladder (MailerLite, beehiiv, Ghost, Flodesk, MailCheap) can be ranked. Mailchimp itself cannot, above 500 contacts, until you read the tier.

## Mailchimp alternatives for nonprofits

Mailchimp alternatives for nonprofits start with the discount you can actually get, then the list size.

Mailchimp's help article About the Nonprofit Discount, checked 25 November 2026, offers 15% to verified nonprofits and charities. It is not retroactive. Add an EIN in the United States before you apply. Mailchimp may ask for a 501(c)(3) letter. International groups send a website link and may be asked for more. The request is a billing contact form. Fifteen percent off an unpublished tier is still an unpublished tier. Read the contact price first.

Constant Contact's pricing page says nonprofits get 20% off when they prepay for six months, or 30% off when they prepay for 12 months. There is no discount on monthly billing. A United States group sends a 501(c)(3) or 501(c)(6) determination letter. Buy the prepay plan only after Constant Contact confirms the rate. The starting list prices before that discount are Lite $12, Standard $35, and Premium $80 a month.

A nonprofit under 10,000 subscribers that only sends a newsletter can stay on Kit Free at $0, which is less than 15% off any paid Mailchimp tier. A nonprofit that needs landing pages, a short automation, and a published ladder can price MailerLite Comfort, including the 30% nonprofit rate MailerLite documents on its own nonprofit page, against the discounted Mailchimp tier. Do not stack those discounts in your head as if one vendor honors the other.

If the organization sells memberships or tickets inside the email tool, Ghost or a paid beehiiv plan may be the fit even at a higher monthly number, because the 15% Mailchimp discount does not add a membership site.

## How to cancel Mailchimp and export your contacts

Cancel the subscription only after the export is in your hands. Mailchimp's help splits "pause" from "delete." Pause keeps the data. Delete removes audiences, campaign archives, and reports, and the account cannot be restored. These steps are from [Pause or Cancel Your Account](https://mailchimp.com/help/close-an-account/), [View or Export Your Contacts](https://mailchimp.com/help/view-export-contacts/), and [Export and Back Up Account Data](https://mailchimp.com/help/export-back-up-data/), checked 25 November 2026.

Export the audience first.

1. Click Audience.
2. If you have more than one audience, choose it in the Audience drop-down.
3. Click Export audience.
4. In the confirmation, click Export CSV. Mailchimp emails you when the file is ready.

Owners and Admins can export an audience. A segment, tag, or group exports from that view with Export segment or the matching control. The file may be a ZIP with separate CSVs for subscribed, unsubscribed, non-subscribed, and cleaned contacts. Exports stay available for 30 days under More options, Export history. The contact CSV does not include open and click rates, purchase history, or signup source. Download an email report separately if you need those numbers.

For a wider backup, use Manage my data. Profile icon, Account, Settings, Manage my data. Check the data types, pick a time frame, and click Export Data. You get one account export per 24 hours. The ZIP can include audiences and the HTML of regular emails. It does not include billing receipts, deleted audiences, or the content of websites, landing pages, and ads.

Then decide whether you are pausing or deleting.

Pause, if you might come back: profile icon, Account and billing, Settings, Manage my plan, Pause my plan. Choose 3 or 6 months. Mailchimp allows 2 pauses every 12 months. You can still edit emails and manage the audience. A Websites plan stays active. Billing for the marketing plan stops for the pause. Restart from the banner when you return, and turn automations back on.

Delete, if you are done: the same Manage my plan screen, then Delete my account. You may see suggestions or a support prompt. Continue to delete, confirm your password, type DELETE, and click Permanently delete. Pay any outstanding balance first. After this, the audiences are gone. The CSV on your computer is the list.

Import only people who agreed to the next sender's mail. Do not import cleaned or unsubscribed addresses as active. Journeys and templates do not come along. Write the welcome message on the new tool before the first campaign. MailCheap's import help accepts a Mailchimp CSV. Send a test to yourself before the list gets an issue.
