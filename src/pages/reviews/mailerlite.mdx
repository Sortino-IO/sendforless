## Comfort and Power

Free is $0, 250 active subscribers, 2,500 emails, two seats, three automations, one website, one landing page. It is a starting account.

Comfort starts at $12 a month at 500 subscribers ($10.80 on the annual effective rate, which is 10% off). Emails are 10 times the tier ceiling, whether you are at the bottom of the tier or the top. On the up-to-1,000 tier that is 10,000 emails. Paid plans add paid newsletters, multivariate tests, logo removal, and more automations (50 on Comfort).

Power starts at $25 ($22.50 annual) and removes the email cap, up to 200,000 subscribers, subject to fair use. Enterprise is custom above that.

| Subscribers, up to | Comfort | Emails on Comfort | Power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 500 | $12 | 5,000 | $25 |
| 1,000 | $19 | 10,000 | $39 |
| 5,000 | $49 | 50,000 | $69 |
| 10,000 | $89 | 100,000 | $129 |
| 25,000 | $179 | 250,000 | $239 |
| 100,000 | $439 | 1,000,000 | $489 |

A weekly send to 5,000 people is about 21,700 emails and fits Comfort’s 50,000. A daily send to the same list is about 151,700 emails and does not. Power at $69 is the daily plan at that tier. MailerLite counts active subscribers. Anyone active during the billing cycle counts even if you delete them before the renewal date. Clean the list early if you are near a tier.

## Who should stay

Stay if you send a regular newsletter and you use more than the send button: landing pages, a modest site, automations, or a paid newsletter inside MailerLite. At 10,000 subscribers, Comfort is $89. beehiiv Lite at that size is $139, and Lite includes a different growth stack. If you are not using beehiiv’s ads or recommendations, Comfort can be the smaller bill for a similar “email plus pages” job.

Stay if you are under 250 subscribers and Free still sends. Do not buy Comfort to escape a $0 plan that fits.

The comparison with Mailchimp, including the contacts Mailchimp counts and the prices we will not estimate, is [MailerLite vs Mailchimp](/compare/mailerlite-vs-mailchimp/).

## Who should look elsewhere

Look at **Kit Free** if you only broadcast and you are under 10,000 subscribers. You give up MailerLite’s larger automation set and its site limits. You pay $0.

Look at **MailCheap** if the weekly send is the product and you do not need paid newsletters or multivariate tests. For a weekly letter to 10,000 subscribers, Growth is $39 (about 43,300 emails) against Comfort’s $89. For a weekly letter to 25,000 subscribers, Pro is $79 (about 108,300 emails) against Comfort’s $179.

Look at **beehiiv or Ghost** if the publication, the recommendations, or the member site is the product. They cost more than Comfort at most of these sizes. That can still be the right bill. The exits are [MailerLite alternatives](/alternatives/mailerlite/).
