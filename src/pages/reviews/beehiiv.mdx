## Who is beehiiv for?

beehiiv is for a writer or a small newsroom that wants the newsletter, the site, and the growth tools in one login. The recommendation network is on Free. Paid subscriptions, automations, A/B tests, custom domains, and the ad network start on Lite. The ad network is not part of the trial and is not in every region, according to beehiiv’s feature notes.

It is a weak fit if you want the lowest possible invoice for a weekly broadcast. Kit is $0 up to 10,000 subscribers for that job. It is also a weak fit if members need a theme you control. That is Ghost.

A solo writer will not feel the seat limits. A small team will, and may land on Pro for seats and branding removal rather than only to send the issue.

## What do you pay for beehiiv?

Free is $0 up to 2,500 subscribers. Lite starts at $59 a month ($49 on the annual effective rate) at the tier for up to 1,000 subscribers. At 4,000 subscribers you pay the up-to-5,000 price: Lite $109 a month, or about $95 if you prepay the year. Pro is higher at every bucket and is not required to get the 0% take rate. Stripe still applies. beehiiv quotes 2.9% + $0.30.

Sends are unlimited, including on Free. A second issue does not move the tier. Adding subscribers does.

Every bucket, monthly and annual, is on [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/). Comparisons: [beehiiv vs Substack](/compare/beehiiv-vs-substack/), [MailCheap vs beehiiv](/compare/mailcheap-vs-beehiiv/), and [beehiiv vs Kit](/compare/beehiiv-vs-convertkit/).

## What you trade

You trade a subscriber bill for a bundle: recommendations, ads on paid plans, a website, a podcast with download caps, and paid subscriptions at 0% platform fee. You do not get Kit’s sequence builder as the center of the product, and you do not get Substack’s reader app. Those are different networks.

If paid revenue is a few hundred dollars a month and the list is already past 2,500, Substack’s 10% can be the smaller bill. The arithmetic is [beehiiv vs Substack](/compare/beehiiv-vs-substack/).

If you want out of the tier and you still need the paywall, read [beehiiv alternatives](/alternatives/beehiiv/) before you export. MailCheap can import the CSV and cannot bill the paid readers.

## If the job is only the send

At 4,000 subscribers and one issue a week, beehiiv Lite is $109 and MailCheap Starter is $19, provided the month stays inside 20,000 emails. A month with five weekly sends is 20,000 emails before welcome mail and tests. MailCheap will not replace recommendations or the ad network.
