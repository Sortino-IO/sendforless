## What does a newsletter platform review include?

Prices and limits come from each vendor's pricing page and help docs. Inbox rates are outside these reviews.

MailCheap is an affiliate relationship. That is on the review and in the bar at the top of the site. The commission does not add paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests to the product. The method is [how we compare prices](/how-we-compare/).
