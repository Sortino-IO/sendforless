## How a review is scored

We read the vendor’s pricing page, plan table, and help docs. We do not invent inbox rates or a growth lift. When a behavior is described, it is their documentation, dated, and you can open the same page. The method, including what we refuse to measure, is [how we test](/how-we-test/).

MailCheap is an affiliate relationship. That is disclosed on the review and in the bar at the top of the site. The commission does not add paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests to the product, and it does not turn a $19 plan into the right plan for a writer who needs those things. sendforless is not MailCheap and does not speak for them.

## MailCheap

The hosted product starts at $19 a month for 20,000 emails and 10,000 subscribers. There is no free hosted tier and no trial. Yearly billing costs 10 months’ price. Every email counts, including tests and welcomes. The score in the review is 4 out of 5 for that sending job, and an explicit pass for anyone building a paid newsletter or a recommendation business on top of the tool.
