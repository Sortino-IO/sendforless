## Price by scenario

| Situation | Kit | beehiiv |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 subscribers, broadcast only | $0 Free | $0 Free |
| 1,000 subscribers, sequences or automations | $39 Creator | $59 Lite (automations are not on Free) |
| 4,000 subscribers, broadcast only | $0 Free | $109 Lite |
| 10,000 subscribers, broadcast only | $0, at the cap | $139 Lite |
| 25,000 subscribers, weekly | Check current pricing | $199 Lite |
| Paid subscriptions | Available on every plan. Kit charges 3.5% + 30¢ per sale, card processing included. | 0% platform fee on Lite and Pro, plus Stripe. Not on Free. |

Both charge by subscriber count on paid plans and don't limit sends. A second issue does not change the beehiiv tier.

Full tables: [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/) and [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/).

## Network or sequences

- **Recommendations and ads.** beehiiv’s recommendation network exists on Free. The ad network and paid recommendations are paid-plan features, and the ad network is not in every region. Kit has a Creator Network. Paid plans get a larger share of recommendations. Earning from paid recommendations needs a paid Kit plan.
- **Sequences.** Kit Creator is the visual automation product. beehiiv automations start on Lite, with a cap of 10 live automations. If the business is a tagged welcome series, Kit is the tool built for that. If the business is a publication homepage plus boosts, beehiiv is.
- **Websites.** Both ship pages. beehiiv’s stack includes a podcast with download caps and a community. Kit’s pages are closer to landing pages and a newsletter site.
- **Commerce.** Both can sell. Do not migrate a working checkout to save a tier until the new checkout has taken a test payment.

We did not measure which recommendation network brings more subscribers. Check your signup source report to see which one brings readers.

## Who should pick which

Pick **Kit** if you are under 10,000 and you broadcast, or if sequences and a creator CRM are the business. Stay on Free until a sequence has a job.

Pick **beehiiv** if you want the ad network, recommendations as the growth plan, or paid subscriptions with a 0% platform fee and you are past 2,500. Who beehiiv suits: [beehiiv review](/reviews/beehiiv/).

Pick **MailCheap** only when you do not need either of those products and you are paying for a weekly send. At 10,000 subscribers, Growth is $39 a month for about 43,300 emails. It has no referral program and no ad network. The review is [MailCheap](/reviews/mailcheap/).
