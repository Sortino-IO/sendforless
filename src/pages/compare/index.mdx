## How to read a table

We use a weekly cadence unless the comparison is about daily sending. Emails are subscribers times issues per week times 4.33, rounded up. A month with five send weeks is higher. On MailCheap, welcome emails and tests count on top of that.

Under 2,500 subscribers, beehiiv Free is $0. Under 10,000, Kit’s free plan covers broadcasts. Substack’s platform fee is $0 until readers pay, and then it is 10% plus Stripe. MailCheap is the lower paid row when you are past those caps, the cadence fits a published plan, and you do not need paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests.

Prices we could not read say “check current pricing” and are not ranked. The rules are on [How we compare prices](/how-we-test/).
