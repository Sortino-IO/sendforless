## What the tables hold

We pick a weekly cadence unless the page is about daily sending, count emails as subscribers times issues per week times 52 divided by 12, and map that volume onto each vendor’s published plan. The winner is not always the lowest number. Under 2,500 subscribers, beehiiv Free is $0 and a hosted MailCheap plan is $19. Under 10,000 subscribers, Kit’s free plan covers broadcasts. Substack’s platform fee is $0 until readers pay, and then it is 10% plus Stripe.

MailCheap is the cheap paid row only when you send on a schedule, you are past the free caps, and you do not need paid subscriptions, ads, a referral program, or A/B tests. That limit is repeated in the matchups, not saved for a footnote. Prices and the rules for placeholders are on [how we test](/how-we-test/).
