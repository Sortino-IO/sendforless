## Price by scenario

Checked 24 November 2026. Kit details are on [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/). Mailchimp’s starting prices are from its plan pages. Higher Mailchimp tiers: [check current pricing](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/).

| Situation | Kit | Mailchimp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 200 contacts, a few sends a month | $0 Free | $0 Free, if you stay under 250 contacts and 500 sends |
| 1,000 subscribers, weekly broadcast | $0 Free | Check current pricing. Standard starts at $20 for 500 contacts. |
| 1,000 subscribers, and you need a sequence | $39 Creator | Check current pricing. Automations on Essentials are short. Standard is the fuller automation plan. |
| 4,000 subscribers, weekly broadcast | $0 Free | Check current pricing |
| 10,000 subscribers, weekly broadcast | $0, at the free cap | Check current pricing |
| 25,000 subscribers | Check current pricing. Free has ended. | Check current pricing |

Kit’s billing help uses $59 a month as the Creator price for up to 3,000 subscribers. Pro is $79 a month at 1,000 subscribers on the pricing page. We do not have a verified Creator dollar for 10,000 or 25,000 subscribers.

A weekly 10,000-person letter is about 43,300 emails. MailCheap Growth is $39 for 75,000 emails and 40,000 subscribers. That is a verified paid price for the same send, if you do not need Kit’s sequences. Five send weeks at 4,000 subscribers are 20,000 emails, which is all of MailCheap Starter before welcomes.

## What you give up

**Stay on Mailchimp** if you use customer journeys tied to a store, ads audiences you already sync, or a team that lives in Mailchimp. Newsletter tools do not import those automations.

**Move to Kit Free** if the Mailchimp account is a weekly letter, the list is under 10,000, and you can live without visual automations. You gain forms, tagging, and broadcasts at $0. You do not get Mailchimp’s commerce journeys.

**Pay for Kit Creator** when a sequence has a job you can name: a course pitch, a welcome series you will not send by hand. Looking at the automation screen is free. Publishing a sequence is not.

Mailchimp counts unsubscribed contacts until you archive them. Export and archive people you will never mail before you decide the bill is the reason to leave.

## Who should pick which

Pick **Kit** for a creator newsletter under 10,000 subscribers that broadcasts, sells a digital product, or needs sequences. The full plan notes are [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/). Other exits are [Kit alternatives](/alternatives/convertkit/).

Pick **Mailchimp** when the account runs more than the letter. The longer exit guide, written from Mailchimp’s side, is [Mailchimp alternatives](/alternatives/mailchimp/).

Pick **MailCheap** when you are past Kit’s free cap, or you are paying Mailchimp for stored contacts, and the job is a scheduled send without a paywall inside the tool. Growth is $39 at 10,000 weekly subscribers. The review is [MailCheap](/reviews/mailcheap/).
