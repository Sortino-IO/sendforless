## Flat rate or a contact database

Flodesk Email is a design-led sending tool: templates, workflows, A/B subject lines, and a price that ignores list size. It is a poor fit if you need Mailchimp’s audiences, store automations, or a free plan that actually sends.

Mailchimp is the better fit when the account is the customer database. A newsletter that happens to live there can still be expensive, because you pay for contacts you store. Archive unsubscribed people before you decide Flodesk’s $46 is the discount. It might be, once you have read your own tier. It is not, at 500 contacts, where Standard starts at $20.

Mailchimp’s send limit scales with the contact tier (12 times the contact limit on Standard). Flodesk does not meter sends on Email or Everything. A daily newsletter does not raise Flodesk’s price. It can raise Mailchimp’s tier if the send multiple is too small.

## When MailCheap costs less than Flodesk

MailCheap is a third option for the send, not for Mailchimp’s store. Starter is $19 for 20,000 emails. Growth is $39 for 75,000. Growth covers a weekly list up to about 17,300 subscribers in an average month, or 15,000 if a five-send month also has to fit. Above that, MailCheap Pro is $79 and Flodesk's $46 is lower.

MailCheap does not include Flodesk’s A/B tests or Mailchimp’s commerce journeys. It has no free hosted plan.
