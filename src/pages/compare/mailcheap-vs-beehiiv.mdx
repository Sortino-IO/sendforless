## Price by scenario

Month-to-month list prices, 24 November 2026. beehiiv’s tiers are from [beehiiv.com/pricing.md](https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing.md). MailCheap’s plans are from [mailcheap.ai/help/plans](https://mailcheap.ai/help/plans).

| Situation | MailCheap | beehiiv |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 subscribers, weekly, no paid features | $19 Starter | $0 Free |
| 2,500 subscribers, weekly | $19 Starter | $0 Free, at the cap. Lite is $79/mo if you need paid features. |
| 4,000 subscribers, weekly | $19 Starter for about 17,300 emails. Five issues are 20,000, before welcomes. | $109 Lite |
| 4,000 subscribers, daily | About 121,300 emails. Pro is $79. | $109 Lite. The price does not move when you add issues. |
| 10,000 subscribers, weekly | $39 Growth (about 43,300 emails) | $139 Lite |
| 25,000 subscribers, weekly | $79 Pro (about 108,300 emails) | $199 Lite |
| 100,000 subscribers, weekly | $149 Scale (about 433,300 emails) | $469 Lite, at Lite’s cap |

Annual billing does not close the 4,000-subscriber gap. beehiiv Lite at the 5,000 bucket is $1,140 a year. MailCheap Starter on a yearly plan is 10 times $19, or $190, if the email cap still fits.

Under 2,500 subscribers, recommending MailCheap over beehiiv Free means paying $19 for a shorter product. We would not do that when Free already sends the issue.

## What each product includes

| Capability | MailCheap | beehiiv |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free hosted plan | No. Hosted plans start at $19/mo. | Yes, up to 2,500 subscribers. |
| Pricing | Email allowance and a subscriber cap. | Subscriber bucket. Unlimited sends. |
| Paid subscriptions | No. | Yes on Lite and Pro, 0% platform fee. Stripe applies. |
| Ad network | No. | Yes on paid plans, not in every region, not in the trial. |
| A/B tests | No. | Yes on paid plans. |
| Recommendations | No referral program. | Yes, including on Free. |
| Automations | Yes, on a paid plan. | Start on Lite. |
| Website | A newsletter site and custom domain on paid plans. | Website, podcast, and community, with limits by plan. |

A writer who sells a paid tier inside the newsletter is not choosing between $19 and $109. MailCheap cannot bill those readers. A writer who sells sponsorships directly, and only needs the broadcast, is choosing whether Lite’s tools earn more than the price gap.

## Who should pick which

Pick **beehiiv** if you want paid subscriptions, the ad network, recommendations, or a site and podcast in the same product. Pick it if you are under 2,500 and Free is enough. The full ladder is [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/). Who the product is for, without another tier table, is the [beehiiv review](/reviews/beehiiv/).

Pick **MailCheap** if you send on a schedule you can estimate, the estimate and a five-week month both fit a published plan, and you do not need the features in the “No” column above. The longer write-up is the [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/).

If you are leaving beehiiv and you still want a paywall, read [beehiiv alternatives](/alternatives/beehiiv/) before you export. MailCheap documents a CSV import from beehiiv. Paid subscription state does not move, because MailCheap cannot bill it.
