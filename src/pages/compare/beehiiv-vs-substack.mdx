## Price by scenario

Substack, from its help center (updated 29 October 2026, re-read 24 November 2026): publishing is free at any list size. Paid subscriptions cost 10% of each payment, plus Stripe. The article quotes 2.9% + $0.30 for US cards and 0.7% for Stripe Billing on recurring charges. A custom domain is $50 once. In-app prices on iOS can be higher if you choose to cover Apple’s fee.

beehiiv, from its pricing file on 24 November 2026: Free is $0 up to 2,500 subscribers and does not include paid subscriptions. Lite at the 5,000 bucket, which is what 3,000 or 4,000 people pay, is $109 a month or about $95 on the annual rate. The full grid is [beehiiv pricing](/pricing/beehiiv/).

| Situation | Substack | beehiiv |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 free subscribers, nothing for sale | $0 | $0 on Free |
| 4,000 free subscribers, nothing for sale | $0 | $109 Lite. Free has ended. |
| 4,000 subscribers, $400 a month paid revenue | $40 | $109 |
| 4,000 subscribers, $2,000 a month paid revenue | $200 | $109 |
| 25,000 subscribers, $8,000 a month paid revenue | $800 | $199 Lite |

Sends are unlimited on both. beehiiv’s price moves when you add free subscribers. Substack’s platform fee does not. The 0% figure on beehiiv is the take rate on revenue.

Annual beehiiv billing moves the crossover. Lite at the 5,000 bucket is about $95 a month if you prepay, so 10% overtakes it at about $950 of monthly subscription revenue rather than $1,090.

## What you are comparing

- **Recommendations.** Both have them. They are different networks. We did not run a growth test of either one.
- **Ads.** beehiiv has an ad network on paid plans, not in every region, and not in the trial. Substack is not that product.
- **Automations.** beehiiv puts them on Lite and Pro, not on Free. Substack is a simpler editor. If you run onboarding sequences, beehiiv is closer to Kit.
- **Custom domain.** beehiiv includes one on paid plans. Substack charges $50 once.
- **Take rate.** 0% on beehiiv paid plans, 10% on Substack, Stripe on both.

If “website” means a theme you control, add Ghost to the comparison. See [Ghost vs Substack](/compare/ghost-vs-substack/).

## Who should pick which

Pick **Substack** if paid revenue is under the Lite bill, or if you have not turned on paid subscriptions and you do not want a monthly fee for free readers. Pick it if you are starting this week and you do not yet know if anyone will pay.

Pick **beehiiv** if paid revenue clears the tier, or if you want the ad network and automations enough to pay Lite before that crossover. Pick Pro for branding, seats, or the higher ceiling, not because the 10% math requires it.

Pick **neither** if you want a membership site you theme yourself (Ghost), sequences and a creator CRM (Kit, free to 10,000 for broadcasts), or a send with no marketplace (MailCheap, from $19, and only when a free plan does not already fit). Migration notes are in [Substack alternatives](/alternatives/substack/) and [beehiiv alternatives](/alternatives/beehiiv/).
