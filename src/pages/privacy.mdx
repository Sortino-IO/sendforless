## What we collect

You can read every page without giving us your name, email address, or a newsletter subscription. We do not run a mailing list on this site, and we do not ask you to create an account.

If you email [hello@sendforless.com](mailto:hello@sendforless.com), we receive what you put in that message, including your address. We use it to answer you or to correct a price. We do not sell that email, add it to a marketing list, or pass it to MailCheap. Delete requests go to the same address.

The site is hosted as static files. A host’s ordinary server logs can include an IP address, a browser type, and the page requested. We do not use those logs to build a profile, and we do not sell them. Hosting can run on Vercel. If it does, Vercel’s own processing is described in Vercel’s privacy policy.

## Cookies and analytics

Pages load Google Tag Manager, which includes Google Analytics. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives typical usage data: the page you view, a rough location from your IP address, and device and browser information. We use that to see which guides are read.

When you click a link to MailCheap, we record that click: the address of the link, the words on the link, and the page you were on. We do not record whether you bought a plan. The calculator runs in your browser. The subscriber count you type is not sent to us as its own report.

We do not sell analytics data. Google’s processing is described in [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can block the tag with your browser or with an extension that blocks Google Analytics.

## Affiliate links

A click on a MailCheap affiliate link takes you to MailCheap. From that moment MailCheap’s site, cookies, and privacy policy apply. The link lets MailCheap attribute the referral to this site. It does not identify you to us. We do not receive your MailCheap account, list, or payment details.

Other outbound links go to the companies named in the sentence. Their policies apply on their sites.
