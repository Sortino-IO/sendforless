## Email

Write to [hello@sendforless.com](mailto:hello@sendforless.com). There is no account system and no web form behind this page, because a form that pretends to deliver mail and does not is worse than an address.

We aim to read editorial mail. We do not promise a deadline. If you are writing about a live sending outage, the platform’s own support is the channel that can see your account. We cannot.

## Corrections

The useful correction names the page, the figure, and the official URL that disagrees. “Your beehiiv number is wrong” is a start. A link to the pricing bucket is a correction we can check. Our method and the date we last read each source are on [How we test](/how-we-test/).

## What we cannot do

We cannot change a MailCheap plan, refund a charge, import a list, or see why a campaign paused. We are not MailCheap. The same is true for beehiiv, Kit, Ghost, Substack, Mailchimp, and MailerLite.

We also cannot add your product to a roundup because you asked, in exchange for a fee. Recommendations follow the [method](/how-we-test/), and the funding relationship is only the MailCheap affiliate program described in the [disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
