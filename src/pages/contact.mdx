## Email

Write to [hello@sendforless.com](mailto:hello@sendforless.com). There is no account to sign in to on this site.

We read editorial mail. We do not promise a reply by a set date. If a send is failing right now, use that platform’s support. They can see the account. We cannot.

## Corrections

A useful note names the page, the figure, and the official URL that disagrees. Include the link to the vendor's pricing page. The method and the date we last read each source are on [How we compare prices](/how-we-test/).

## What we cannot do

We cannot change a MailCheap plan, refund a charge, import a list, or see why a campaign paused. We are not MailCheap. The same is true for beehiiv, Kit, Ghost, Substack, Mailchimp, MailerLite, and Flodesk.

We don't accept payment to add products to a roundup. Recommendations follow the [method](/how-we-test/). The funding relationship is the MailCheap affiliate program described in the [disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
