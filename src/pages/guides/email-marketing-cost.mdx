<h2 id="often">Email marketing prices when you send more than once a week</h2>

Email marketing pricing plans fall into two groups. One group charges for people or for a flat product, and a second issue in the week does not move the invoice. Kit, beehiiv, Ghost, Flodesk's paid plans, and Substack's $0 publishing fee are in that group. Kit's pricing page says there is no sending limit on these plans. Prices here are the monthly plan, except where MailCheap and MailerLite count each email. beehiiv's pricing FAQ says every plan includes unlimited sends. Ghost's pricing page says unlimited email sends. Flodesk's paid plans include unlimited emails.

The other group counts emails. MailCheap's allowance is the plan cap. MailerLite Comfort's allowance is 10 times the subscriber tier. A daily letter, seven issues a week, is about seven times the weekly email count.

<h2 id="models">Email marketing pricing models</h2>

These eight products use four pricing models. The model tells you which number will move next year. These are monthly list prices unless the sentence says the charge is annual.

**Per subscriber, or per member.** beehiiv Lite and Pro, Ghost Publisher, MailerLite, and Kit's paid plans move when the list crosses a bucket. You do not pay more for a second issue. You do pay more when the list grows, even if you send less often. Kit bills confirmed subscribers. The billing help, checked 25 November 2026, uses $39 a month as the Creator price up to 1,000 subscribers and $59 a month as the Creator price up to 3,000. Annual Creator is 12 months for the price of 10, so the $39 tier is $390 for the year. Pro was $79 a month at 1,000 subscribers on [kit.com/pricing](https://kit.com/pricing) on 24 November 2026. Above 1,000 subscribers, check current pricing on Kit.

**Per email, with a subscriber cap.** MailCheap Starter is $19 for 20,000 emails and up to 10,000 subscribers. Growth is $39 for 75,000 emails and 40,000 subscribers. Pro is $79 for 200,000 emails and 100,000 subscribers. Scale is $149 for 500,000 emails and 250,000 subscribers. A larger list on a quiet cadence can stay on a small plan. A small list on a daily cadence cannot.

**A flat sending price.** Flodesk Email is $46 a month on monthly billing, or $456 a year, at any list size, with unlimited subscribers and sends. Everything, which adds checkout, is $99 a month or $948 a year. Flodesk Email has one price at any list size.

**A percent of reader revenue.** Substack has no paid plan. The cost of email marketing on Substack shows up when readers pay: 10%, plus Stripe. At $1,000 a month of subscription revenue the platform fee is $100. At $5,000 it is $500. At $10,000 it is $1,000. Stripe is extra. Details and the $50 custom domain are on [Substack pricing](/pricing/substack/). Publishing stays $0 at 10,000 subscribers and at 100,000 until readers pay.

Mailchimp is a contact tier. Free is 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with a daily cap of 250. Essentials starts at $13 a month and Standard at $20, both for up to 500 contacts, as read on 24 November 2026. Above 500 contacts, check current pricing on [Mailchimp's pricing page](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/). Unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts count until you archive them.

Kit's free plan is best for broadcasts under 10,000 subscribers when sequences can wait. MailCheap's published plan is often the lower paid bill for a weekly send past those free caps, when the paywall lives somewhere else. Ghost's higher bill can still fit a membership site. The [platform guide](/guides/newsletter-platforms/) goes through those situations. If you already pay one of these, the switch pages are [Mailchimp alternatives](/alternatives/mailchimp/), [Kit alternatives](/alternatives/convertkit/), [beehiiv alternatives](/alternatives/beehiiv/), [Substack alternatives](/alternatives/substack/), [Ghost alternatives](/alternatives/ghost/), and [MailerLite alternatives](/alternatives/mailerlite/). Pair pages for the same choice include [MailerLite vs Mailchimp](/compare/mailerlite-vs-mailchimp/), [Flodesk vs Mailchimp](/compare/flodesk-vs-mailchimp/), [Kit vs Mailchimp](/compare/convertkit-vs-mailchimp/), [beehiiv vs Kit](/compare/beehiiv-vs-convertkit/), and [Ghost vs Substack](/compare/ghost-vs-substack/).

<h2 id="hidden">What the monthly price does not include</h2>

These are the charges that show up beside it.

**The other term.** A monthly price and an annual price are different products in the checkout. beehiiv Lite at 10,000 subscribers is $139 if you pay each month and $1,428 if you pay for the year. You do not get the $119 figure as a monthly withdrawal. Ghost Publisher at 10,000 members is $105 a month or $1,056 for the year.

**Sales tax on the software.** Kit's billing help says some US states are charged sales tax on the plan, based on the billing address. The $39 example in that article becomes $41.34 with a 6% tax. Other vendors may charge tax too. The list prices here are before that tax.

**Stripe and any platform percent.** That fee applies only when readers pay. The worked numbers are on [newsletter monetization](/guides/newsletter-monetization/).

**A domain.** Substack charges $50 once for a custom domain. beehiiv includes custom domains on paid plans, not on Free. MailCheap includes your own domain on a paid plan. Ghost's pricing page lists a custom website domain on Starter and lists the custom sending domain, the one on the email itself, on Publisher and not on Starter. A registrar's annual fee for the name is outside all of these invoices.

**A dedicated IP.** Flodesk lists one as an add-on. Flodesk does not publish a price for it.

**Seats and extra mail.** A second login can be included or billed. Read that line on the vendor page if the team is why you are buying. Welcome emails, confirmation emails, and tests do not change an unlimited plan. On MailCheap they consume the cap. On MailerLite Comfort they consume the 10-times allowance.

**The feature you turn on in month two.** Kit Free does not include sequences. beehiiv Free does not include automations, paid subscriptions, or the ad network. Ghost Starter does not include paid subscriptions. Flodesk's free plan does not send. The $0 row is wrong the week you need the missing feature. Price the paid plan in the same table, not as a surprise.

The software bill does not include payment processing or freelance help. Agency retainers are a separate cost and are not covered here.

<h2 id="free">Free plans, and the first paid tier</h2>

Email marketing can cost $0 for a long time. A missing feature can still force a paid plan.

Kit's free plan, described in a help article updated 23 September 2026 and checked 25 November 2026, is $0 up to 10,000 subscribers, with unlimited broadcasts, forms, and landing pages. Sequences, visual automations, rules, and RSS need Creator or Pro. Past 10,000 subscribers you have to pay to keep sending. Commerce, including a paid newsletter, is on the free plan, at 3.5% plus $0.30 per USD sale. That fee applies only when readers pay.

beehiiv Free is $0 up to 2,500 subscribers and unlimited sends. Leave it early only if you need automations or paid subscriptions, which means Lite. At 2,501 subscribers the price is the up-to-5,000 Lite bucket, $109 a month, unless you move.

Substack is $0 to publish at any size. Substack has no paid plan. It is 10% of whatever readers pay, plus Stripe.

MailerLite Free is 250 active subscribers and 2,500 emails a month. The first Comfort tier in the pricing file is $12 a month at 500 subscribers, or $10.80 a month on the annual schedule. At 1,000 subscribers Comfort is $19. The free plan can sell one digital product. It cannot send a paid newsletter.

Mailchimp Free stops at 250 contacts and 500 sends. The next published starts, for up to 500 contacts, are $13 and $20. After that, check current pricing.

Flodesk Free does not send. The first sending plan is Email at $46 a month or $456 a year. There is no sending tier between $0 and $46.

Ghost has no $0 hosted plan. The first plan is Starter at $18 a month or $180 a year, without paid memberships. Publisher at $35 a month, or $348 a year, is the first plan that can charge members, at up to 1,000 members.

MailCheap has no $0 hosted plan. The first plan is Starter at $19 a month, or $190 for the year. Use it when the free plans above no longer fit the send, and when you do not need them for paid subscriptions, an ad network, or A/B tests inside the tool. If Kit Free still sends the broadcast, Starter is an extra $19, not a requirement. The [Kit pricing](/pricing/convertkit/) page and the [MailCheap review](/reviews/mailcheap/) are the two write-ups to read before that switch. The head-to-head with a subscriber tier is [MailCheap vs beehiiv](/compare/mailcheap-vs-beehiiv/).

For the eight products together, with the situation each one fits, use [newsletter platforms](/guides/newsletter-platforms/). For the steps before you are looking at an invoice, use [how to start a newsletter](/guides/how-to-start-a-newsletter/).
