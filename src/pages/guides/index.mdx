## Starting, and the bill that comes later

A new letter does not need a paid plan on day one if Kit, beehiiv, or Substack will send it for $0. Kit’s free plan covers broadcasts up to 10,000 subscribers. beehiiv Free stops at 2,500. Substack has no monthly fee until you charge readers, and then it keeps 10%. MailCheap’s hosted plans start at $19 and are the wrong first account if a free plan already sends the issue.

Once a list exists, the same arithmetic decides a switch. Weekly volume is subscribers times 52 divided by 12. Tools that charge per email, or that cap emails at a multiple of the tier, care about that number. Tools that charge per subscriber do not. The [home page calculator](/#calculator) runs the comparison. [How we test](/how-we-test/) lists which prices are official and which are still placeholders.
