A free plan is the right start when it already sends. Kit's free plan covers broadcasts up to 10,000 subscribers. beehiiv Free stops at 2,500. Substack has no monthly platform fee until readers pay, and then it keeps 10% plus Stripe. Those figures were checked on 25 November 2026. Annual prices are a separate charge.

Pay when the free cap is gone, or when the free plan withholds the feature. Sequences on Kit, paid subscriptions on beehiiv, and any send on Flodesk's free plan are in that group. Flodesk's pricing page says the free plan does not send. MailCheap and Ghost(Pro) have no hosted free plan. Ghost Starter is $18 a month and cannot sell memberships.

<h3 id="costs">Costs</h3>

The cost guide prices a list at several sizes and at more than one send frequency. A weekly planning estimate is subscribers times 4.33. Tools that meter emails can move up a plan when you add a second issue. Tools that charge for subscribers, or a flat rate, often do not. The [pricing hub](/pricing/) is the index of the vendor tables. The [calculator](/#calculator) runs the same list prices at a list size you type.
