## Where to start

If the list is still small, a free plan is often enough. Kit’s free plan covers broadcasts up to 10,000 subscribers. beehiiv Free stops at 2,500. Substack has no monthly fee until you charge readers, and then it keeps 10%.

When the list no longer fits those caps, or you are already paying a bill that climbs with subscribers, compare the monthly cost of a real send. A weekly issue is about 4.33 sends a month.

MailCheap Starter is $19 a month for 20,000 emails and 10,000 subscribers. Use it when that allowance covers the way you send, and when you do not need paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests inside the tool. The [calculator](/#calculator) runs the same comparison at your numbers. [How we compare prices](/how-we-test/) lists the official sources behind the dollars.
