## What we cover

sendforless is an editorial site for people who publish a newsletter: independent writers, creators, and small teams choosing a platform or thinking about a switch.

The [home page calculator](/#calculator) maps a list size and a cadence onto published prices. Longer pages cover Mailchimp, MailerLite, Kit (formerly ConvertKit), beehiiv, Ghost, Substack, Flodesk, and MailCheap. A practical starting piece is [how to start a newsletter](/guides/how-to-start-a-newsletter/).

## How we research

We read each vendor’s pricing page, plan table, and help docs. When a page describes a limit, that description comes from those sources, dated. We do not run inbox tests. We don't give scores, because we haven't run hands-on tests.

The method, the source list, and the prices we leave off the site are on [How we test](/how-we-test/). Prices were checked on 24 November 2026 unless a paragraph names an older source date, such as MailerLite’s pricing file.

## How the site is funded

Some links to MailCheap are affiliate links. If you start a paid MailCheap plan through one of them, we may earn a commission. You pay MailCheap’s price either way. The link is labeled, and the bar at the top of every page says the same thing in short form.

We do not take payment for a higher placement or to leave out a limitation. We recommend MailCheap only when your regular send fits one of its plans and you don't need a feature that only a free plan or a competitor has. Read the full [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

Links to every other platform go to that company’s site. They are not affiliate links from sendforless.

## Independence

We are not MailCheap. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or speaking for MailCheap. We are not beehiiv, Kit, Ghost, Substack, Mailchimp, MailerLite, or Flodesk. Trademarks belong to their owners.

We do not publish private discount codes. When a vendor’s yearly price is lower than twelve monthly payments, we describe that as annual billing.
