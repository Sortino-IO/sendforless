## What this is

sendforless is an editorial site about newsletter platforms. The home page compares public list prices. The guides go one level deeper: Mailchimp, MailerLite, Kit, beehiiv, Ghost, and Substack, plus a review of MailCheap and a practical piece on [how to start a newsletter](/how-to-start-a-newsletter/).

We write in English, for people who send email on purpose and want the invoice to match the job. The design is dark and plain because the subject is a price, not a launch.

The method, including what we do not measure, is on [How we test](/how-we-test/). Prices on the site were checked on 24 November 2026 unless a paragraph names an older source date, such as MailerLite’s pricing file.

## How the site is funded

Some links to MailCheap are affiliate links. If you start a paid MailCheap plan through one, we may earn a commission. You pay the same price you would pay without the link. The code is a single configuration value in the site, currently `analyst`, on URLs such as `https://mailcheap.ai/pricing?via=analyst`.

We do not accept payment for a higher placement, a softer limitation, or a deleted paragraph. MailCheap is recommended only where a regular, price-sensitive sender does not need a free hosted plan, paid subscriptions, an ad network, a referral program, or A/B tests. Those limits are repeated on the home page, in the review, and in the roundups, including when they make MailCheap the wrong row.

Links to every other platform go to that company’s site and are not affiliate links from sendforless.

The full wording is the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## What we are not

We are not MailCheap, and we are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or speaking for MailCheap. We are not beehiiv, Kit (ConvertKit), Ghost, Substack, Mailchimp, or MailerLite. Trademarks belong to their owners. A page titled “MailCheap review” is our review, not their marketing.

We do not run a coupon site. We do not publish private discount codes. Annual billing, when a vendor publishes it, is described as a billing term, not as a promotion we arranged.

Questions about a guide go to [hello@sendforless.com](mailto:hello@sendforless.com). Questions about your MailCheap account go to MailCheap. We cannot see your bill, your list, or your deliverability.
