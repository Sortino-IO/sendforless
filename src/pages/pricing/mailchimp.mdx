## Free, Essentials, Standard, and Premium

The help article describes four marketing plans.

**Free** includes up to 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with a daily send limit of 250. One audience, one seat, a limited template set, and basic reporting. You can try landing pages and forms. Past 250 contacts, live and test sends are held until you upgrade or remove contacts.

**Essentials** starts at $13 a month for up to 500 contacts and can store up to 50,000. The monthly send limit is 10 times the contact limit. The help lists 3 audiences, 3 seats, single email automations, flows of up to 4 steps, all email templates, A/B testing, and scheduled sends. The Essentials lander also says the Mailchimp logo can be removed.

**Standard** starts at $20 a month for up to 500 contacts and can store up to 100,000. The send limit is 12 times the contact limit. The help lists 5 audiences, 5 seats, expanded automation flows, advanced segmentation, custom-coded templates, and multivariate testing.

**Premium** can store up to 200,000 contacts. The base includes 10,000 contacts. The send limit is 15 times the contact limit. Audiences and seats are unlimited, and phone support is included. Above 200,000 contacts, Mailchimp says it will customize a plan.

Pay As You Go is an alternative to a monthly plan and uses the Essentials feature set. The credit price is on Mailchimp's pricing page.

Above 500 contacts, read the dollar on Mailchimp's pricing page.

## Who counts as a contact

A contact is any member of an audience. Subscribed, unsubscribed, and non-subscribed people count. Archived, cleaned, and deleted people do not. The contact total is everyone stored across audiences. If you go over a paid limit during the month, Mailchimp does not stop the account. It adds a charge. Lower the audience and keep it lower for a full billing cycle if the spike was temporary.

That rule is why a Mailchimp bill and a MailerLite bill can describe different headcounts. MailerLite bills active subscribers. See <a href="/pricing/mailerlite/">MailerLite pricing</a>.

## Send limits

Each email to one person is one send, including tests. At a 10,000-contact Standard limit, 12 times 10,000 is 120,000 sends. A weekly letter to those 10,000 people is about 43,334 emails on the 4.33-week estimate. The allowance can fit. The price of that contact limit is still on Mailchimp's page.

A five-send month is subscribers × 5. At 10,000 people that is 50,000 emails before tests, still inside a 120,000 allowance. The dollar for that tier is on Mailchimp's pricing page.
