## Starter, Publisher, and Business

**Starter** is $18 a month or $180 a year, up to 1,000 members. The pricing page lists one staff user, design settings rather than custom themes, one newsletter, and no paid subscriptions. There is no custom sending domain.

**Publisher** is the membership plan. The pricing page lists 3 staff users, custom and marketplace themes, paid subscriptions, a 0% transaction fee, and a custom sending domain. Three premium tiers are included. Newsletters: 3.

**Business** is $239 a month from 1,000 through 10,000 members ($2,388 a year, $199 a month). The pricing page lists 15 staff users. At 25,000 members the price file is $319 a month or $3,192 a year.

**Custom** is the band past the published member steps. The slider's last stop points you to Ghost's team.

Whitelabel branding is listed on every hosted plan. Recommendations and Ghost Explore are listed on every plan. This page does not measure whether Explore replaces another network.

<p>The price file also has 7,500 and 75,000. 7,500 members pays the 10,000 price only if you round the wrong way. It does not. 7,500 is its own band: Publisher $95 a month or $948 a year ($79). Use the next band that is greater than or equal to your member count. Starter exists only at 1,000.</p>

## The 0% fee

Ghost's cut is $0 on Publisher. You still pay the member tier in a quiet month, and you still pay Stripe. Members can be exported, and an import can include stripe_customer_id when the subscription already lives in your Stripe account. That portability is described in Ghost's import help. It is not a promise that a Substack paid reader arrives as a paid Ghost member.

## Compared with a percent

At $1,000, $5,000, and $10,000 a month of subscription revenue, Substack's platform fee is $100, $500, and $1,000, before Stripe. Ghost's platform fee is $0, plus whichever row in the table matches your member count. The worked comparison is <a href="/compare/ghost-vs-substack/">Ghost vs Substack</a>. Substack's own numbers are on <a href="/pricing/substack/">Substack pricing</a>. Other exits are on <a href="/alternatives/ghost/">Ghost alternatives</a>.

If you do not need member login or a theme, a sending plan can be the smaller bill. MailCheap is one of those, from $19 a month, and it does not sell memberships.
