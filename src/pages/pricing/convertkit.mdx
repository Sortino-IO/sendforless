## Free, Creator, and Pro

**Free** is the plan for broadcasts. Kit’s help article lists unlimited forms and landing pages, tagging, segmentation, API access, A/B tests on broadcasts, and five recommendation slots. Paid recommendations and earning from them need a paid plan. You can turn recommendations off.

**Creator** adds visual automations, email sequences, rules, and RSS. It also removes Kit branding and adds a second user. The price scales with confirmed subscribers. Kit bills active confirmed subscribers, not emails sent. When you pass a tier, Kit’s help says the account moves up automatically. A downgrade to a lower tier is something you start from billing settings.

**Pro** adds reporting by provider, subscriber scoring, Facebook audience sync, collaborative editing, a referral program, and unlimited users. The only Pro price Kit publishes as a fixed starting figure, on the page we read on 24 November 2026, is $79 a month at 1,000 subscribers. [Check current pricing](https://kit.com/pricing) for any other list size.

Kit’s pricing FAQ says the Creator and Pro trial is 14 days and does not require a card. A refund is available within 30 days of the first payment, according to the billing help. We do not treat either of those as a reason to pick the product. The renewal price is the one to budget.

## Where the bill changes

Kit publishes these figures in writing. Other list sizes are on Kit’s pricing page.

| Subscribers, up to | Published price | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | Creator $39/mo. Pro $79/mo. | Pricing page, 24 November 2026, and billing help for the $39 tier |
| 3,000 | Creator $59/mo (per Kit’s billing help) | Billing help, read 24 November 2026 |
| 10,000 | $0 on Free, for broadcasts | Kit free-plan help, updated 23 September 2026 |
| Above 3,000 on a paid plan | Check current pricing | [kit.com/pricing](https://kit.com/pricing) |

Free still covers 4,000 and 10,000 subscribers if you only broadcast. The paid price matters when you publish a sequence, or when the list passes 10,000 and Free stops sending.

Kit does not charge you for unsubscribers, according to the billing article. Cleaning the list can qualify you for a lower tier, but the downgrade is not automatic.
