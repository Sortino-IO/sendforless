## What a price on this site means

We use month-to-month USD list prices. Annual billing is described when the vendor publishes it, as a yearly charge or as an effective monthly rate, not as a coupon. Short promotions are ignored.

Email volume, when a tool meters sends, uses subscribers times issues per week times 4.33, rounded up. A month with five send weeks is higher. At 4,000 subscribers, five weekly issues are 20,000 emails, before welcome emails and tests.

beehiiv, MailCheap, Ghost(Pro), MailerLite, Flodesk, Substack’s 10% fee, Kit’s free cap, and the Kit prices named on the Kit page come from those companies’ own pages, checked 24 November 2026. Mailchimp above 500 contacts, and Kit above the stops we could read, are not given a dollar here. The source list is [how we test](/how-we-test/).
