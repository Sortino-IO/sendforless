## What does newsletter platform pricing include?

We use month-to-month USD list prices. Annual billing is described when the vendor publishes it, as a yearly charge or as an effective monthly rate, not as a coupon. Short promotions are ignored.

Email volume, when a tool meters sends, uses subscribers times issues per week times 4.33, rounded up. A month with five send weeks is higher.

beehiiv, MailCheap, Ghost(Pro), MailerLite, Flodesk, Substack’s 10% fee, Kit’s free cap, and Kit's Free, Creator, and Pro prices come from those companies’ own pages, checked 25 November 2026. Mailchimp above 500 contacts, and Kit above 3,000 subscribers on Creator, have no dollar on this page. The source list is [How we compare prices](/how-we-compare/).
