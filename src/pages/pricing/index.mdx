## What a price on this site means

We use month-to-month USD list prices. Annual billing is described when the vendor publishes it, as a billing term, not as a coupon. Short promotions are ignored, including introductory percentages that expire. Email volume, when a tool meters sends, is subscribers times issues per week times 52 divided by 12, rounded up, so a month with five send days is already in the estimate.

A figure is either a verified list price, a slider capture we could not re-read as a static table, or a placeholder. Mailchimp’s mid-tier Standard amounts are placeholders. Kit Creator bands above the published $39 start at 1,000 subscribers are a slider capture. beehiiv, MailCheap, Ghost(Pro), MailerLite, and Substack’s 10% fee are cited from those companies’ own pages. The full source list is [how we test](/how-we-test/).

The [calculator on the home page](/#calculator) applies those rules to a list size and a cadence. It is the right tool when you want six bills at once. This section is for the page-length explanation of one vendor’s grid.

## beehiiv pricing

beehiiv is the pricing page we publish in full because beehiiv publishes every Lite and Pro bucket, monthly and annual, in a file generated from the same data as the pricing page. Free stops at 2,500 subscribers. After that, the price is the bucket you have crossed, and sends are not the meter.
