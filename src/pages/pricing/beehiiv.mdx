## How much does beehiiv cost on Free, Lite, and Pro?

The plan picks the features. The subscriber tier picks the price inside Lite and Pro.

**Free** is $0. It includes up to 2,500 subscribers and unlimited sends, a website, a podcast within 5,000 downloads a month, and the recommendation network. It does not include automations, paid subscriptions, the ad network, A/B tests, or custom domains. One publication, one seat, 30 days of analytics, three segments.

**Lite** adds paid subscriptions at a 0% platform fee, the ad network (not in every region, and not during the trial), automations, polls, A/B tests, custom domains, and surveys. beehiiv’s feature table caps Lite at 100,000 subscribers, 2 seats, 3 publications, and 10 live automations. Branding stays on.

**Pro** adds branding removal, up to 250,000 subscribers, 10 seats, dynamic content, and more publications. At monthly list prices, Pro is $50 to $230 a month more than Lite in the same bucket. That gap is branding, team limits, and the ceiling.

**Enterprise** is custom. There is no public price.

[beehiiv’s October 2026 plan notes](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/beehiiv-pricing-2026) map older names: Launch became Free, Scale became Lite, and Max became Pro. Existing customers move at the first renewal on or after 1 November 2026. Annual customers keep the prior rate until that term ends.

## Yearly and monthly

At 1,000 subscribers, Lite monthly is $59 and the annual effective rate is $49, which is $588 a year instead of $708. You save $120 and you prepay. At 10,000, Lite monthly is $139 and the annual total is $1,428 instead of $1,668.

Annual billing is the ordinary yearly price on beehiiv’s page. It is a poor fit if you are still testing whether you will use the ad network. MailCheap’s yearly rule is different: you pay for 10 months and get 12. Ten times $19 is $190. Compare the dollars, not the percent off.

## When the tier is worth paying

Pay for Lite if you will charge readers, use the ad network, or run automations you do not already have somewhere else. The 0% take rate is the comparison with Substack once subscription revenue is larger than the Lite bill.

Stay on Free if you are under 2,500 and Free already sends the issue. Move to Pro for a Pro reason: branding removal, more seats, dynamic content, or the higher subscriber ceiling. If the only job is the Sunday send, Kit Free (under 10,000) or MailCheap is the closer price. The roundup is [beehiiv alternatives](/alternatives/beehiiv/).
