## What are the MailerLite plans?

The MailerLite plans are Free, Comfort, Power, and Enterprise. MailerLite pricing steps up with active subscribers, not with a feature you unlock one at a time.

**Free** is $0. It includes 250 active subscribers, 2,500 emails a month, 2 seats, 3 automations, 1 website, 1 landing page, 3 signup forms, and 1 digital product or booking. API and MCP access does not include sending.

**Comfort** starts at $12 a month at 500 subscribers ($10.80 annual). It adds unlimited templates, 50 automations, 10 websites and landing pages, 10 forms, 5 digital products, paid newsletters, logo removal, and a custom domain. The email allowance is 10 times the tier ceiling. A dedicated IP is an add-on.

**Power** starts at $25 a month at 500 subscribers ($22.50 annual). Emails are unlimited through 200,000 subscribers, subject to fair use. Automations, sites, forms, and products are unlimited. Multiple triggers on one automation are a Power feature. Seats are unlimited. Support adds 24/7 chat.

**Enterprise** is custom above 200,000 subscribers.

Plan names changed on 16 June 2026. Growing Business is Comfort. Advanced is Power. The <a href="/reviews/mailerlite/">MailerLite review</a> uses the same file.

## What counts as a send

One email to one person is one send. A campaign to 500 people is 500 sends. The allowance resets on the billing date. Comfort's cap uses the tier ceiling, so 200 subscribers on the up-to-1,000 tier still get 10,000 emails, not 2,000. If Comfort runs out, you upgrade to Power or wait. Free locks campaigns and automations above 250 active subscribers.

Paid accounts step up to the next tier automatically and charge the card. You get a notice before you hit the limit.
