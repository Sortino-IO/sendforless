## How much does Flodesk cost?

**Free** is for building forms, landing pages, and a link-in-bio page. It is not a place to send the newsletter. When a trial ends and you do not pick a paid plan, Flodesk says the account moves to Free and keeps the subscriber data. Sending stops until you upgrade.

**Email** is the sending plan: unlimited subscribers, unlimited emails, workflow automations, A/B tests, branding removal, and one seat (the account owner). This is the plan to compare with other newsletter tools if you are not selling through Flodesk.

**Everything** adds checkout: sales pages, payment plans, upsells, subscriptions, and sales tax processing, plus two more seats (three total). Flodesk says it does not add a transaction fee on top of Stripe. Checkout is available for businesses in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, New Zealand, and Euro Area countries. Email marketing itself is available more widely, and the USD price is the same.

A dedicated IP is an add-on, not part of the flat rate. Flodesk does not publish its price.

## What does Flodesk cost as the list grows?

Flodesk’s monthly price stays at $46. MailCheap’s price stays low while a weekly send fits Starter ($19, 20,000 emails) or Growth ($39, 75,000 emails), then steps up. A weekly estimate uses 4.33 sends. Five send weeks are subscribers times 5, before welcome emails and tests.

For a weekly letter that fits Growth, MailCheap is $39 against Flodesk’s $46, and you give up Flodesk’s A/B tests. Flodesk is the lower invoice once the weekly volume needs MailCheap Pro ($79) or above. A daily letter hits MailCheap’s email cap sooner, so the crossover happens at a smaller list. Run that cadence in the [calculator](/#calculator).

Kit Free is still $0 up to 10,000 subscribers for broadcasts, which is less than both. beehiiv Lite at 10,000 subscribers is $139. Those are different products: Kit if you only send the newsletter, beehiiv if you want its ad network and recommendations.

## If you already pay Flodesk

From November 10, 2026, Flodesk’s help article says the two unlimited plans are the only paid plans new members can buy. If you are on an older Lite, Pro, or Everything tier, that plan keeps its price until you change it. You cannot return to a legacy plan after you leave it. The public page for a new account is the flat rate above. Older tier grids are not listed here, because they are not for sale.

Prices were read on [flodesk.com/pricing](https://flodesk.com/pricing) and in the help article [Flodesk pricing: the new Unlimited plans](https://help.flodesk.com/en/articles/14551297), edited November 19, 2026. Both checked November 25, 2026.
